# Notes for a Talk

Good writing is basically just rewriting. At the end of the day, the first draft tells you what you think, and the second draft tells the reader.

I want to argue that the best editing tools are invisible. They should feel like a sharper pencil, not a second author. A careful writer reads every sentence aloud and cuts the words that only fill space.

Keep the structure simple: three points, one story, and a clear ending that people can really carry home. The end result should be a talk that sounds like a person, not a slide deck.
