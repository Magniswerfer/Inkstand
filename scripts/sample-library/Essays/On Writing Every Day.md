# On Writing Every Day

Writing every day is less about discipline than about lowering the cost of starting. The blank page is only frightening when it is rare. Once it becomes ordinary, it turns into a room you walk into without thinking.

## Start small

The first sentence does not have to be good. It has to exist. Most days I begin with a line about the weather, or the coffee, or the sound of the street, and by the third sentence I have forgotten that I was warming up.

- Write before you read anything else
- Stop in the middle of a sentence, so tomorrow begins on its own
- Keep a list of half-ideas for the days that start empty

## Keep the tools quiet

A good tool disappears. It does not ask you to choose a font, a theme, or a layout before you have written a word. It gives you a page and a cursor, and then it gets out of the way.

> The work is the words. Everything else is furniture.

That is why I write in plain text. The files are mine, they open anywhere, and they will still open in twenty years. #craft #habits

## What changes

After a few months, the habit stops feeling like effort. You notice sentences in the wild. You start carrying a notebook again. Some days the page stays quiet, and that is fine too: showing up was the point.
